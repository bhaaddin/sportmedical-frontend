## Decisions

* The five questions are four blocks: the fifth (quick actions) is the top action bar's. The same answer holds on a phone (the "+" and the search in its top bar, "Nový pacient" on Pacienti).
* "Top 3 items of the attention model" is read as the top 3 AREAS (one row per group with its count); the individual cases are in the hover pop-out, because three cases from one group would crowd out the others.
* The week is the Prague week (Monday to Sunday) and counts bookings that stand or happened (no cancellations, no no-shows) - the same rule as "Dnes".
* "Who is next" is the first booked visit that has not ended; once the booked visits are over the block says "Dnes už nikdo další" instead of inventing one.
* Settings' group list stays as a panel beside the rail on desktop (the plan said it stays); the rail never has children - "where you are" inside an area is the drawer's job.
* No fixed `max-width` on the page: `fit.rules.test.ts` forbids it and the owner asked for the freed width; at 1920 the blocks simply have more air.

## Risks and open points

* The icon rail has no labels; the tooltips and the drawer are the labels. A first-time person may not know the logo is Přehled (its tooltip says so). Owner's call if he wants a one-time hint.
* The week chart's "today" number is shown only for today (hover gives the rest) - clarity over completeness (R18). Say if he wants all seven numbers.
* "Čeká" shows two names and "+N"; the full waiting room is `/dnes`.
* The quick actions are not on the Přehled itself; if the owner expected a visible "Nová objednávka" button on the page, it is the top bar's filled button, one eye-jump above.
* Not run: the real-stack check against a backend with real club requests/bookings (the busy runs use in-page answers for the two lists; everything else is the real API).
* A "mock-up first" for the owner (R18 plan row) was not made as a separate artefact - the built screen with `design/stage4/after-prehled-*.jpg` is the mock-up; judge it before pushing.
