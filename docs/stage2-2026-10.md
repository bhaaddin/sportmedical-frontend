## R2 - the selector lists služby

- Reproduced (`design/stage2/r2-before-desktop-week.png`): chips `Ordinace 1 / Diagnostika / InBody / Balíčky` are the names of the
  four CALENDARS; one click on a chip hid that calendar (so a click from "all" showed three), two services side by side needed three
  clicks, and the hint talked about calendars.
- Data check (`scripts/stage2/data-dump.mjs`, dev): there is one active calendar per služba (`clinicServiceId` 1:1) and no calendar per
  činnost; the činnosti belong to the služba (`clinicServiceId` on the činnost). So in the dev data nothing had to be hidden. The code
  handles the case the owner describes: `serviceGroups` (`board/serviceGroups.ts`) groups the calendars by `clinicServiceId`; a služba
  with several calendars is ONE chip and ONE column, its first calendar (sortOrder, name) is the primary and the others ride in its
  column (`alias`, used by the week model, the move targets and the clash drawing) - bookings of an extra calendar stay on screen and
  nothing is deleted or deactivated. A calendar that runs no služba (`clinicServiceId` null) is its own group, named after itself,
  because hiding it could hide bookings. Whether the LIVE data has per-činnost calendars could not be checked from here (no live login);
  if it has, they have a `clinicServiceId` of an existing služba and fold away by themselves, if they have none they stay visible as
  their own chips (the data decision for those is the owner's).
- Behaviour: the chips carry the služba's name and colour; from "Všechny" one click shows ONLY that služba, a click on a second adds a
  column to every day (the split of fix 2.4, at most three), a click on a shown one hides it, hiding the last one is "Všechny" again
  (`toggleGroupSet`). The činnosti of the shown služby are listed inside as colour pills (not clickable, not calendars; desktop and
  tablet, hidden on a phone). The column heads, the month tile counts and the compact day chips use the služba's name and colour; the
  calendar's own name ("Ordinace 1") is still what a booking card, the agenda and the dialogs say.
- Tests: `serviceGroups.test.ts` (grouping, extras, no-service calendar, clicking), `WeekBoard.calendars.test.tsx` (chips named by
  služba, one click one služba, a second adds a column, an extra calendar is one column and keeps its bookings),
  `scripts/stage2/r2-flow.mjs` (the real app: chips, one / two / three clicks, pills, "Všechny").

## R6 - holidays and the locked day

- Reproduced (`r6-before-desktop-*.png`): 28. 10. 2026 in the dark theme was a hatched grey-blue day with the hour lines drawn through
  it; weekends used the same hatch; the holiday colour was the server's beige default `#E7CBA9` and the mini calendar painted the
  number of EVERY closed day (weekends too) in it.
- Now: `planner/model/dayLock.ts` is the one place. A holiday (or clinic closure) is the fixed red `#C62828` (white ink 5.6:1), a weekend /
  day off is the neutral grey `#3F4852` (white ink 9.3:1). The fill is an inline literal (`--lock-fill` / `--lock-ink`), so the
  accent shift - which only moves the stylesheet's accent-chrome colours - never touches it. A locked day is ONE opaque block over the
  whole column (`.board-shade.board-lock`: solid fill, `background-image: none`, no border, above the hour lines / finer grid /
  decoration), with a lock icon and "Svátek · <name>" / "Zavřeno" (+ the reason of a closed day in a smaller line). The bookings a
  locked day still holds are written as text lines INSIDE the block (a locked day draws no card, window, lunch or club window, but
  nobody booked there disappears). The same two colours drive the day heading, the month tile (lock + label), the mini calendar
  (holiday number on the red disc, closed number grey, legend `SVÁTEK` / `ZAVŘENO`), the phone strip and month list and the compact day
  buttons.
- Settings: `holidayColor` stays the API field; the old beige default (and a stored colour that fails the guard) is read as the red
  (`effectiveHolidayColor`, applied in `readCalendarDisplay`). The field is now "Barva svátků" with a guard (`holidayColorProblem`):
  AA text on it, visible on the dark AND the light surface, not a grey (HSL saturation >= 35, or it would merge with the weekend); a
  failing colour is refused with a sentence and cannot be saved.
- Tests: `dayLock.test.ts` (contrast table: 6 accents x 2 themes, ink AA, visible on both surfaces; guard; the red is independent of
  the accent), `WeekBoard.test.tsx` "a locked day (R6)" (lock look, no card / window / lunch on it, bookings listed as text, stacking
  order and the stylesheet rule), `CalendarDisplayPage.audit.test.tsx` (guard-passing colours), `MiniDayButton.clubs.test.tsx`, and the
  Playwright pixel test `scripts/stage2/r6-pixels.mjs`: for 6 accents x dark / light the holiday and the weekend column are sampled
  below their label (128x408 px each): 0 pixels differ from the fill, computed ink/fill 5.62:1 and 9.29:1, `background-image: none`,
  opacity 1; the same measurement on the neighbouring OPEN column finds 52 224 differing pixels (the check can fail).
- Not changed: the planner has no complete light theme yet (Stage 3 / R15e); the "light" runs only prove the fill does not move.

## R4 - one person does one thing at a time

- Settings (`Nastavení > Kalendář a mřížka`, `SingleWorkerCard`): the switch "Pracovník dělá jen jednu věc najednou" and the exception
  list (active accounts), saved to `booking-slots` with only the two fields (a PUT that leaves a field out keeps the stored value). The
  card exists only when the server's answer carries the fields; an older server gets no switch.
- Refusals: `BookingApiError` now keeps the problem `code` and `details`. `worker.busy` (409) becomes a Czech sentence built from the
  structured detail ("Pracovník je v tuto dobu zaneprázdněn: Základní diagnostika v kalendáři Diagnostika, 15. 12. 09:00-10:00."): the
  clashing booking (calendar, činnost, time) and never a patient; a club block is named by its činnosti. For `settings.clinic.manage`
  (and only when `overridable`) there is the override with a required reason - in the booking dialog and in a refused drag (a dialog
  of its own); the retry carries `overrideReason`; `403 worker.busy.override_forbidden` is shown as "smí jen správce".
- The clash is drawn in the other column (`board/workerClash.ts`): with the rule on and two služby side by side, the worker's other
  commitments (bookings that are not cancelled, club windows) are locked, hatched slots with the reason. The worker of a calendar on a
  day is the rota's (`workerUserId` of the day preview); no worker = the calendar is the resource = no clash; people on the exception
  list draw nothing.
- Real backend (`scripts/stage2/real-s2.mjs r4`, run after run on fresh days): settings save and read back; the rota gives the same
  worker to two calendars (schedule exception); a booking in Diagnostika, the same time in InBody -> `409 worker.busy` with details
  naming Diagnostika and no patient name; the clash is drawn in the InBody column with the reason; the offers (availability, staff
  starts) list no InBody start inside the busy hour; a drag of a second InBody booking onto it -> the real refusal opens the dialog; the
  owner's override moved it, the reason is in the history; at 11:00 the worker is busy too; after `workerExceptions: [owner]` the same
  booking is accepted (201).
- Not wired (honest): change of činnost (`PUT .../activity`) has no override by contract and shows the generic conflict text; club
  block / club order calls (they live on the club planner and the order pages, which this stage did not change) were not given the
  override query parameter; the move dialog and the booking dialog are the two places that offer the override.

## R13 - club holds in the main calendar

- What already existed: club windows were drawn hatched in the week, the month tile listed them as text lines and the phone strip had
  dots; a click opened the order popover. What was missing: the order code, the lock, the hold state, the refusal, the release, the mini
  calendar and the setting.
- Now (contract fields `orderCode`, `playersMissing`, `windowEmpty`, `holdExpired`, `seats`, `registered`, `clubOrderId`, all optional):
  the week/day window shows the lock, club, order code and the state in words (`Čeká na hráče` / `Obsazeno` / `Hold vypršel`; an expired
  window has dashed borders), the month tile draws the club day as a hatched, locked bar in the club's colour with the same words, the
  phone agenda row carries code and state, the mini calendar marks the days with a hatched bar per club (ONE `club-blocks` read per
  shown month; its name carries club, order and state). The club popover (a click opens the order's summary, with "Otevřít objednávku"
  -> `/clubs/objednavky` with `openOrderId`) shows the state and seats and offers "Uvolnit nevyplněné" (and "Uvolnit jen vypršené" when
  expired) for `bookings.edit`, with a confirmation and the server's result in words; it exists only where the server sent hold data.
- Refusal: marking free time over a hold (or booking / blocking / adding to a club from the menu) says "Rezervováno pro klub X" and opens
  no menu; a dragged booking shows the same text on its ghost and in the toast.
- Settings: `Nastavení klubů` has "Držet čas klubu do X dní před termínem" (0-365, 0 = never, default 7), hidden on a server without it.
- Real backend (`real-s2.mjs r13`): the club blocks of the dev data carry the contract fields (order code, `playersMissing`, `holdExpired`);
  week / month / mini calendar / popover draw and say them; the release answers and a second release changes nothing; the hold setting
  reads 7 and saves.
- Not done: nothing is deleted on its own - an expired hold is only flagged, as the contract says; the order list's "hold" column and
  notifications are the club pages' business.

## R17 - the lunch

- Reproduced: the OBĚD band was a pointer-less hatch; clicking it did nothing; the weekly lunch could be neither moved nor removed for
  one day. Friday: the dev data has a 12:00-12:30 break on Friday for every calendar and the band IS drawn on Friday (the backend
  agent's finding holds); the planner draws a lunch only when every open calendar shares one, so a single calendar with another or no
  Friday break hides it - that is data, and the seed now writes the default lunch. The frontend did not invent a lunch for calendars the
  server says have none.
- Now: with a server that has the lunch routes (recognised by the Stage 2 fields of booking-slots, then confirmed by one list call; an
  older server is never asked and the band stays as it was) the band is a button and can be dragged. Pop-out: Přesunout (a time box,
  any time of the day, the same length, refused over a booking on screen and by the server over any booking / club), Smazat jen dnes,
  Objednat pacienta sem (the booking dialog on exactly the lunch slot; the booking goes to `POST .../appointments/into-lunch`, which
  removes THAT day's lunch and books - a quick registration cannot be made this way) and Vrátit (moved) / Vrátit oběd (removed). A moved
  lunch reads "Oběd · přesunut" and says where it was; a removed one leaves a dashed "Oběd · smazán dnes" ghost where it was. A drag shows the
  landing time (red with the reason when refused) and writes on release. The day's lunch belongs to the clinic (the day mark is the lunch
  all open calendars share), so a change is written to every open calendar, not only to the služby on screen.
- Permissions follow the contract: moving / deleting / restoring need `bookings.edit`, booking into the lunch `bookings.create`.
- The default lunch: Nastavení has the clinic's default (`lunchFrom` / `lunchTo` of the calendar display settings, 12:00-12:30). The
  per-weekday override is NOT built: the contract has no such endpoint ("if the contract provides it").
- Real backend (`real-s2.mjs r17`): see the final report numbers; the checks cover the band as a button, five working days with Friday,
  the move written to the calendars, the effective lunch + `lunchException` in the preview, the same weekday a week later untouched,
  restore, remove + ghost, the overlap refusal (`lunch.overlaps_booking`), into-lunch (201, that day's lunch removed, no other day
  touched) and the dialog opening on the lunch slot.
