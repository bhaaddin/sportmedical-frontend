Branch `stage/s3b` (from `claude/clean` ef2fb0e), local commits only: nothing pushed, no GitHub, Render or deploy touched.
Everything below was reproduced first with our own headless Chromium (`scripts/stage3b/*`, never the shared browser pane) against the

__RESULTS__

## 1. R5 - every pop-out, both modes, six accents

### What was measured (the audit)

`scripts/stage3b/audit-lib.mjs` reads the **computed** styles of every visible element in the real browser: the text colour (alpha and the
opacity of every ancestor folded in) against the surface behind it (all background layers and gradient stops composited bottom-up, a
gradient judged at its worst stop) - 4.5:1, 3:1 for large text; every inline `<svg>` icon, 3:1; every input/select boundary, 3:1
(WCAG 1.4.11). Disabled controls and hidden things are exempt; a borderless search line inside the command palette is identified by its
icon and placeholder, not by a boundary, and is reported separately.

`scripts/stage3b/audit-popouts.mjs` opens, in dark and light (`--accents`: and for each of the six accents of `src/accent.ts`):

| source | pop-outs |
|---|---|
| DEV gallery `/audit/popouts?kind=` (`src/dev/PopoutGallery.tsx`, behind `import.meta.env.DEV`, never in a production bundle) | Dialog, confirm Dialog, Popover, Menu, Select list, Tooltip, Drawer, Autocomplete list, Snackbar + filled Alerts, Alerts (standard / outlined / filled x 4 severities), form controls (outlined input, multiline, native select, native date, checkbox, switch, chips) |
| the app, opened the way a person opens it | planner: Objednávka (booking dialog), Klub, board settings, user menu, notifications, mini-calendar day tooltip + pinned detail, lunch flyout, empty-slot bubble, Měsíc view; command palette; club list "Nový klub" and club page "Upravit klub" + "Nová objednávka"; patients "Nová objednávka" and "Hledat"; Barvy popovers (služba, činnost); Svátky and Činnosti dialogs |

Native `<input type=date>` pickers and native `<option>` lists are drawn by the browser from `color-scheme` (set per mode by the theme),
so there is no DOM to read; they are in the gallery's form controls only as the field around them.

### What was wrong (found by the audit, then fixed)

* **Outlined and filled `Alert`**: MUI derives the outlined text from `palette.x.light`, which is a translucent tint in the dark theme
  (alpha .14: contrast 1.3:1, invisible); filled alerts took a darkened `main` with black ink (3.7:1). Explicit tone colours now.
* **`Snackbar`**: the dark theme's snackbar was a near-white bar with the cyan "Zpět" action (1.5:1); light mode's was a dark bar with
  the dark-green action (1.7:1). `MuiSnackbarContent` override: glass in dark, ink in light, white/aqua action.
* **White text on the primary button of dark mode** (`color: "#FFFFFF"` on `AppointmentDetail`'s secondary/contained buttons): the
  contrast text of the palette now.
* **Light pastel tones on the dark app** (`DESIGN.tone.*`, 81 uses in 14 files): a dark app showed pale beige/red/green boxes. They now
  read `--sm-tone-<tone>-<bg|fg|line>` (set by the theme per mode, `TONE_CSS`), so a pop-out is tinted glass in dark and the board's
  pastel in light.
* **Planner tooltip / popovers drawn in a fixed dark theme** (`PlanDarkTheme`, literal `#042c3d/#eaf7ff` in `BoardCards`): they follow
  the mode now.
* **Field borders** were 2.3:1 (dark `#22546A`) and 1.2:1 (light `#E2E6EA`): `FIELD_EDGE` (`#4A8BA5` / `#7C858F`) is >= 3:1 on every surface a
  field sits on. The activity colour input, the planner's own selects and inputs follow.
* **Muted text** `#626B75` was 4.05:1 on a selected item; `#5A636D` (4.57:1) everywhere (`DESIGN.muted`).
* Smaller: the "Hodina" selected segment of the board settings (white on `#00A89B`, 2.97:1: the lighter gradient stop is `#00857B`), the mini-day
  tooltip's primary button (stale gradient parse), `NetworkBanner` (white on orange 3.0:1), `QuickPendingCard`, `RuianAddressPicker`.

### One token set, and a guard

`popoutTokens(accent, mode)` in `src/theme.ts` (paper, every paint stop, border, text, muted, hover, selected) is the single definition
of what floats. The MUI paper overrides read it; the theme also writes it to `:root` as `--sm-pop-*` in BOTH modes, which is what the
planner's CSS and its inline styles read (`BoardCards` tooltip). The generated planner light layer (below) gives the planner's own
dialogs the same values.

* `src/theme.popouts.test.ts` - the contrast table: 6 accents x 2 modes x every surface stop (text, muted, accent, hovered and selected
  item, the five tones on their own fill, the filled-alert ink, field boundary) and a check that the CSS variables are the tokens.
* `src/theme.popouts.guard.test.ts` - scans every file that opens a Dialog/Popover/Menu/Snackbar/Drawer/Tooltip/Autocomplete/Popper
  (110+ files) and **fails on any hex / rgb / hsl / `'white'` literal** outside a short allow-list with a reason per entry (a printed
  report window, role category colours, the lunch default). Generated stylesheets are excluded and covered by their own test.
* `src/test/fixtures/theme-default-*.json` (the committed picture of the default theme) were re-taken: the only differences are the
  deliberate ones above (the `--sm-pop-*` / `--sm-tone-*` variables, Alert variants, SnackbarContent, field border, muted text).

## 2. R15e - the whole staff app in light

`src/planner/styles/planner.css` is the generated dark prototype and was NOT touched. The light look is a **generated** layer:

`node scripts/planner-light-css.mjs` reads every planner stylesheet (the generated one and the 13 hand-written component CSS files) and writes
`src/planner/styles/planner.light.css` (~200 kB, loaded on demand, brotli-small): only the declarations that paint, each repeated under
`html[data-sm-mode="light"]` (set on the document root by `AccentRootVars`, staff app only), so in dark nothing matches and dark is
pixel-identical. The translation is by purpose, not blind inversion:

* text: the contrast the colour had on the navy page is kept against white (near-white -> ink `#1A1D21`, accent chrome -> the accent's AA ink);
* glows and shadows: a soft ink shadow, an accent halo at 35 %;
* surfaces: navy -> white / `#EFF1F4` tints, bright accent fills -> the accent, dark teal panels -> a pale accent tint, a white veil -> an ink veil,
  a black scrim -> a light ink scrim, mid greys pushed clear of the unreadable middle;
* a rule that paints its own fill and sets its own text is a PAIR: the ink is re-solved on the translated fill (accent -> ink-on-accent,
  any other fill -> whichever of white/ink reads better); a fill that is a data colour keeps the dark rule's text;
* the added `html[data-sm-mode="light"]` costs the same specificity on every rule, so the cascade between rules (the "repeat the class to win"
  tricks) decides as it does in dark, and every paint declaration is copied (even without a colour literal) so a colourless dark rule can never
  out-rank a translated one.

`planner.light.extra.css` (hand-written, loaded after) holds what a translation cannot know: the page, the closed-day block (a light grey
block; the holiday keeps its strong red), the closed-day numbers in the mini calendar, the booking card as a pale tint of the činnost colour
(`--ev-bg-lt`), field boundaries. The calendar chip icon's ink is computed per colour (`--cal-ink`).

`PlanDarkTheme` (club-planning's popovers) now follows the mode. The Světlý/Tmavý switch is `sm-theme-mode-v2` as before.

Tests: `src/planner/styles/planner.light.test.ts` (the generated file is current, every rule hangs on the light attribute, translation
keeps AA text, masks/urls untouched), `src/AccentRootVars.test.tsx` (the attribute follows the switch and is removed on leaving the staff app).

### Every staff route, light mode - `scripts/stage3b/audit-all-routes.mjs`

Reads the route table of `src/StaffApp.tsx` (a new route is audited without touching the script) and, per route, in one run: the share of the
viewport whose surface is darker than relative luminance 0.1 (must be <= 5 %), text AA, icons and controls 3:1, no document scroll, no sideways
overflow, and "EMPTY-PAGE" if nothing was audited (a blank/crashed page is never a pass).

__ROUTES__

The patient/club portals and the public site draw their own themes (`PublicLayout`, `WebApp`); they do not read `sm-theme-mode-v2` and
`data-sm-mode` is removed when the staff app unmounts, so they are unchanged by design.

## 3. R12 + R15d - colours as settings (Nastavení -> Barvy)

### Default činnost colours (R12)

The server's `effectiveColorHex` is "the služba's shade", so the three prohlídky were three greens, the diagnostika ones three blues, and on the
dark board `#09423B` was nearly invisible ("they do not light up"). The shared helper `activityColor` (`src/colors/activityColor.ts`) is still the
only source; for a činnost with no colour of its own it now returns `defaultActivityColor(id)` (`src/colors/defaultActivityColors.ts`):

* the hue is the činnost's position in the list of ALL činnosti ordered by id (ids are time-ordered: a new činnost is appended, no existing
  colour ever moves; independent of session, služba or viewer), spread by a base-2 van der Corput sequence 0, 180, 90, 270, 45 ... so
  consecutive činnosti - the three of one služba - are as far apart as possible and the first 8 are >= 45 degrees apart;
* lightness is solved per hue to ONE relative luminance (0.17): >= 3:1 on the dark stage `#001421` and on white, white text >= 4.5:1 - the
  same swatch is AA-safe in both modes;
* until the list is known the hue is a hash of the id (the same colour every time), and `activitiesApi.list` / `fetchBlockableActivities` register the ids.

Every consumer already went through `activityColor` / `activityColorOrNull` (board cards, deck, mini calendar liquid and rings, month tile, lists, club
summary "Detaily po činnostech", phone, patient/club/order screens, Ceník); the callers that passed no `id` now do (`categoryOf`, `activityInfoOf`,
`fetchBlockableActivities`, the Barvy and Ceník editors).

Tests: `src/colors/defaultActivityColors.test.ts` (distinct hues for 1..8 činnosti, determinism, append-only stability, AA in both modes for every hue).

### The Barvy section

`/nastaveni/barvy-sluzeb` ("Barvy") - everything is saved the moment it is picked, with palette, custom hex (typed or native picker, checked as typed),
live preview, an AA guard and a reset:

| colour | where it is stored | reset gives |
|---|---|---|
| planner tint (replaces the fixed blue) | `ThemePrefs.plannerTint`, `localStorage sm-planner-tint` (like the accent) | "Sledovat barvu aplikace" (default: follows the accent) |
| služba | `ClinicService.colorHex` (`PUT /api/clinic-services/{id}`) | the palette colour at its position |
| činnost | `Activity.colorHex` (`PUT /api/activities/{id}`, whole činnost) | its distinct default colour |
| kalendář | `Calendar.color` (`PUT /api/calendars/{id}`, whole calendar via `calendarToInput`) | the palette colour at its position |

The planner tint reaches the planner stage only (dark: the prototype's cyan family is shifted to the tint through `--sm-hsl`; light: the generated
layer reads `--sm-planner-accent`, `-ink`, `on-accent`); the rest of the app keeps the accent. The preview shows the tint in both modes.

### Backend: exactly one thing would make the tint clinic-wide

The tint is per browser because `PUT /api/v1/settings/calendar-display` is the whole settings object and has no field for it, and the brief said no backend
change. To share it: add `plannerTintColor` (`#RRGGBB`, nullable, default null = follow the accent) to `CalendarDisplaySettings` (read for anyone signed in, write
`settings.clinic.manage`, validated like `holidayColor`); `ThemePrefsProvider` would read it from `useCalendarDisplay()` when the browser has no tint of its own.

## Open risks / not done

* R16 (segmented ring per činnost, club day as one framed block) is Stage 3 but not in this brief; not built here.
* The first paint of light mode loads the light layer on demand: on a cold cache the planner can show one dark frame before it arrives (the layer is requested as
  soon as the staff app mounts, before the lazily loaded planner chunk).
* Colours drawn by data (a činnost, a calendar) are the owner's: the guard keeps them readable, but a colour deliberately similar to the page is still allowed to be chosen.
