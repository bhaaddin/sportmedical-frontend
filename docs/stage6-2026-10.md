## R16b - the club day as one framed block

- Model (`board/clubFrame.ts`): a drawn club block is the day's windows of one order joined across the lunch (`drawnBlocksOfDay`, stage
  Etapa 12). Each window carries `activityIds` (stage 2 contract); `frameSubs` makes one sub-block per window that names činnosti, with the
  činnost's `activityColor` (catalogue: effective colour, then the služba's), several činnosti in one window = equal hard stripes (never a
  blend), an unknown činnost grey (never another club's colour). A window without named činnosti (older server, unfilled order) has no
  sub-block and keeps the club's hatch. `BoardWindowModel.frame` holds them (px offsets inside the window).
- Board (`WindowBlock`): ONE block, a continuous 2 px solid frame in the club colour (4 px on the left), the club name once, order code,
  lock icon, hold state (`Čeká na hráče` / `Obsazeno` / `Hold vypršel`, expired keeps a dashed frame), the sub-blocks inside, and under the
  words small činnost chips when the block is tall enough (>= 64 px). The lunch gap between two windows stays the club's hatch inside
  the same frame. Click = the existing club popover (the order); booking, marking and dragging onto it are refused exactly as before
  with "Rezervováno pro klub X" (stage 2 code, tests unchanged and green).
- Month tile: the bar is a closed frame with a row of činnost colour segments; mini calendar: the club mark becomes a small framed pill with
  one segment per činnost (from the club-block's `activityIds`); phone: the day entry and the month row list the činnosti with their colour
  dot. The words are in `title` / `aria-label` ("08:00-12:00 Spiroergometrie"), colour is never the only cue.
- Requested / Invited orders hold no time and have no block: they keep the dashed inquiry marker (untouched).
- Data caveat: the dev API in this checkout is an older build that does not send `activityIds` / `orderCode` on time blocks. The browser
  check completes the blocks answer in the browser (Playwright route) with the stage 2 fields; everything else is real dev data. With the
  unmodified dev answer the frame draws without sub-blocks (the fallback above), which is also what an older production API would show.
- Checks: `scripts/stage6/r16b-check.mjs` at 390 / 820 / 1440 - frame solid, club name once per block, sub-blocks present, month tile
  segments, mini frames, phone day + month lists, click opens the popover, the document never scrolls: ALL PASSED. Tests:
  `board/clubFrame.test.tsx` (9: ids to colours, ordering, stripes, ONE item over the lunch, WindowBlock draws sub-blocks / none).

## R15c - extras of the booking flow

All of (a)-(g) are in `src/components/booking/extras/` as pure models with their tests, plus small components that are lazy-loaded
(`DiscountPresetChips`, `SameDayNotice`); the planner chunk does not grow from them.

**(a) discount presets** (`discountPresets.ts`, `DiscountPresetChips.tsx`, `AgreedPriceField` got an `extras` slot). Chips under the price:
`-5 %`, `-10 %`, one `Skupina -N %` per distinct tier of the clinic's group-discount settings (`GET /api/v1/settings/group-discounts`, readable by
anyone signed in; a failing read leaves 5 / 10), and `Zdarma`. A chip FILLS the editable agreed price; the field stays editable; `Vrátit ceník`
resets; pressing the chip that is on resets too. Percent is taken from the LIST price (never compounded on an edited price), whole Kč, half
up, in integer arithmetic (1 250 Kc - 5 % = 1 188 Kc, not 1 187). `Zdarma` = 0 Kc and the booking is refused until a reason is typed (any
0 Kc price, preset or typed); it is not offered in the quick-registration mode where a reason cannot travel with the booking. The reason
goes into the note as the existing "Důvod úpravy ceny" line.

**(b) Nejbližší volný termín per činnost** (`nearestFree.ts`, `nearestFreeSearch.ts`). The server's availability for THAT činnost on the chosen
calendar, from now, 14 days at a time, at most four calls (8 weeks); the first start strictly after now. A link under every činnost in the
list ("choose it and go to its first free start") and one button for the chosen činnost; nothing is booked, the slot card moves, a line says
which date and time, or "Žádný volný termín v příštích 8 týdnech". The calendar-level "Příští volný termín" on the slot card is unchanged.

**(c) same-day notice** (`sameDay.ts`, `SameDayNotice.tsx`). When the picked patient already has a live (not cancelled) booking that Prague day:
"Pacient už má ten den rezervaci: 09:00 Spiroergometrie a 1 další." with "Otevřít kartu pacienta" (new tab, so the booking in progress is not lost).
Warning severity, no button blocks. The day is read from the calendars the user may see; the patient is matched in the answer, no
patient data is put in a URL.

**(d) undo** (`undoState.ts`, `useBoardUndo.ts`, bar in `BoardSurface`). After a drag-move or a cancel a bar "Termín přesunut. 30 s [Vrátit zpět] x"
(or "Rezervace zrušena.") stays 30 seconds. Only the LAST change can be undone (a newer one replaces it). State machine:
`idle -> armed -> running -> idle`, a late or stale click does nothing, dismiss never interrupts a running undo.
- move: `PUT .../time` back to the previous start; the patient is told again only if the move told them. Refusals (the place was taken
  meanwhile, worker busy) are said ("Vrátit zpět se nepodařilo: ...") and the booking stays where it is.
- cancel: see below.

**(e) shortcuts** (`shortcutMap.ts`, `useBoardShortcuts.ts`, `ShortcutsHelp.tsx`). `N` new booking (next free slot, as the toolbar button), `T` today, left /
right previous / next period (a week, or a day in the day view, or a month in the month view), `D` `W` `M` day / week / month, `/` search,
`Ctrl+K` search (that is the existing command palette - it is left alone, never opened twice), `?` the list in a small pop-out (Esc / `?`
/ the button / a click outside closes it). Silent while typing (input, textarea, select, editable, combobox) and while a dialog / menu / listbox
holds the focus; no Ctrl / Alt / Meta chords (browser's); key repeat does nothing; works on non-Latin layouts through the physical key.
Only on the main calendar: the club planner (`useClubPlanner() !== null`) and the demo planner are untouched (the old planner-wide `N`
stays there). Handled keys are consumed in the capture phase so the old `N` does not also fire.

**(f) phone search** (`phoneMatch.ts`, `usePhoneSearch.ts`, `PatientSearch`). A query that is only digits and phone punctuation (>= 4 digits)
is a phone query: `777 123`, `+420777123`, `00420 777 123 456` all match the same row (the country prefix is dropped). The register's server
search is by name only and sends its query in the URL, so a number is NEVER sent: the browser reads the register pages WITHOUT any query
parameter (100 per page, at most 10 pages, one cached scan reused by every keystroke) and matches locally. A register larger than 1 000
patients says "Prohledáno prvních N pacientů - hledejte podle jména" instead of pretending to be complete. The Playwright check asserts
that no request URL contains the digits.

**(g) remembered confirmation channel** (`confirmChannel.ts`). The e-mail link checkbox ("Poslat odkaz na vyplnění vstupního dotazníku") is remembered per
signed-in user in `localStorage` under `sm.booking.confirmChannel.<userId>` as `{"sms":false,"email":true}` - two booleans, nothing
else; off by default, and unreadable / foreign / blocked storage means off. Switching it off removes the key. The SMS box is still
disabled ("SMS zatím nejsou aktivní") so only `email` can be true today; the SMS half of the stored value is ready for when it is.
The link is only sent when the user may register patients (`patients.register`) - a remembered `on` can no longer send a link for
somebody who has no such right.

### What cannot be undone

- A cancel cannot be reopened by the API (a cancelled status has no way out; `Confirm` / `CheckIn` on a cancelled booking throw). Undo of a
  cancel therefore BOOKS IT AGAIN: same patient (or walk-in name and phone), činnost, calendar, start, note and agreed price. It is a NEW
  reservation: the old one stays cancelled in the history; a cancellation e-mail the patient already got is not recalled and the new booking
  may notify again; questionnaire answers and paperwork stay with the old reservation; and if somebody took the slot meanwhile the server
  refuses and it stays cancelled. The toast's tooltip and the success sentence say "nová rezervace".
- Undo is NOT offered (a plain notice says so) for a club term (`partnerName` / club discount), a pending quick registration, and a
  booking with neither a registered patient nor a walk-in name.
- A move whose notification mail was sent: the mail is not recalled; the return notifies again only if the move did.
- Anything after 30 seconds, and everything but the last change.

## Open risks

- Sub-blocks need the stage 2 `activityIds` on time blocks; until the backend carrying stage 2 is deployed the frame is drawn without them.
- The phone search scans up to 1 000 patients in the browser; a bigger register needs a server-side phone filter (a backend change, outside this stage).
- Undo of a cancel is a new reservation (see above); the owner should confirm that is acceptable, otherwise the only honest alternative is to
  hide Undo on cancels.
- The desk's remembered SMS choice cannot be exercised until SMS exists.
